1.9.1. Damage on a character or location is represented by damage counters. Each damage counter normally represents 1 damage; any damage counter intended to represent more than 1 damage counts as a separate damage counter for each 1 damage it represents.

1.9.2. Damage can be placed on a character or location by being dealt to them, put on them, removed, or moved to them. Damage can be taken off a character or location by being removed.

1.9.3. A character or location takes damage whenever damage is placed on it, by any means.

1.9.4. Damage being dealt occurs because of a challenge or a resolving effect. To determine the total amount of damage that occurs, follow the steps listed here in order.

1.9.5. If a character or effect would deal damage and that damage is reduced to 0 when modiﬁers are applied, the character or effect is still considered to “deal” damage. If a character or location is dealt damage and that damage is reduced to 0 when modiﬁers are applied, the character or location “takes” no damage. Example: A player has Merida – Formidable Archer in play. Merida has the ability Steady Aim that reads, “Whenever one of your actions deals damage to an opposing character, deal 2 damage to that character.” The player plays Smash, an action that has the effect, “D eal 3 damage to chosen character.” The player chooses Namaari – Resolute Daughter, a character with Resist +3. Smash deals 3 damage, but Namaari takes 0 damage because of Resist +3. Because Smash is still considered to deal damage, Merida’s Steady Aim triggered ability occurs and is added to the bag.

1.9.6. When a character or location with damage leaves play for any reason, all damage counters on that character or location cease to exist.
