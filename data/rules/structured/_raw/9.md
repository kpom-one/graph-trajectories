9.1. Multiplayer games are played with three or more players. Team games are a subset of multiplayer play that aren’t covered in this document.

9.2. Multiplayer games follow the same rules as two-player games. The following rules are additional rules for multiplayer games. These rules may change the official rule s from above. For multiplayer games, these rules either augment or change the standard rules of Disney Lorcana.

9.3. Some multiplayer game formats use simultaneous turns.
